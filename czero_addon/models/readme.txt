Note from the releaser:
Woo yay, a little story! By me!
Right, this M4 was started as a project to make a black looking Blackhawkdown style M4, and it decided to go into something much better. My dear friend hitman said that it was a really nice model, and after a some good critism from various people at the CDG forum, I decided that this could be much more than a private hack.
So, what else did I do? Well, after making the base model, originally on strykers, Hitman ported it onto some rather lovely DirtyJ anims (and he fixed some bugs), this was all very good, but we noticed that Jennifer had released some really lovely animations. Unfortunately, Hitman was unable to port, so we decided to release with just DJ and Stryker anims.
I spent a while doing the render (I at that point had little experience in that field), and then, due to a stroke of pure idiocy, managed to delete the directory with the render, source files, extra textures - the lot. So I forgot about it for about a week, Chinesewarrior did our sprite, and I was thinking of releaseing with a cheap arsed HLMV screenie (of course, I didn't want to do this , I always like to present my projects).
Now, in reply to a post I made a while back, asking if anybody could help convert to CZ arms, Sammath offered too. So I decided I would get someone else to make a render and let Sammath do the Jen anims (and they are bleedin awesome anims, as you will see).
So I asked s7ok3 to do the render, he told me "tomorrow". Damn, I can't wait that long, I'm an excited fool!
I went ahead and made the render, there were a few problems, the lighting sucked, and i had no nice shell casings.
S7ok3, the next day helped me do these things, by modelling and meshing a great 5.56 round, and fixing the lighting on my Cinema4D file.

I spent all day rendering, by which time, Sammath had done the Jen anims, and I was ready to write the readme.

A quick play in photoshop to make some funky text and preview, (and add a few effects I am too dumb to do in C4D) and I am ready to release!

There are two packages, the Clandeadgoat version, which is released first, and on the CDG boards, but doesn't, however, include Jennifer anims O_o Oh noes! This is because they use CZ arms, which are prohibited on CDG :'(
Then the CSN version, which will be sent to CS-Nation will have all the versions.

This M4 Jen anims ingame are, although I say it myself, the best M4 I have ever used - the sounds are perfect, the size and model is perfect, the origins are great feeling....amazing!

Enough of that self-centered BS, now for the other stuff that goes in a readme.txt (or .nfo for them leet people.)

Installation:

You may have noticed that this has a rather complicated directory structure. It isn't really, just find the <Animname>-models and put that in /cstrike/models and put the contents of <Animname>-sounds into /cstrike/sound/weapons
Finally, put the contents of Sprite-Chinesewarrior into /sprites
You can use the renders for your desktop if you are THAT sad. =P
Easy as pie.
